STATESHIFT / FIELD MANUAL

Know the field.
Keep moving.

Brass diamonds contain data. Concrete blocks break line of sight immediately. Three accepted hits end your run. Threat rises every 50 points. Every 100 points, a Warden replaces all guards and patrols the whole arena. Gain 50 more points to remove it and bring normal patrols back. It outruns you in chase. Break line of sight behind cover to escape.

OPERATOR CONTROLS
